How to read model scores

ML and DL show class YES model scores. QML shows an SVM decision margin, not a probability. Their numerical scores are not directly comparable or calibrated medical risk.

About the quantum model

A four-qubit circuit maps four PCA components into quantum states on a CPU simulator. A classical SVM learns from their similarities. ML and DL use all 15 features. No quantum speed advantage is claimed.
